# ❄️ "SnowPro Core Certification – Introduction"

The **SnowPro Core Certification** is the foundational credential offered by Snowflake to validate your understanding of the **Snowflake Data Cloud platform**. It is ideal for:

- Data professionals
- Engineers
- Analysts
- Architects
- Anyone working with Snowflake in a technical capacity

---

## 🧠 "What It Tests"

The exam evaluates your knowledge across six key domains:

1. **Snowflake Architecture & Features**
2. **Account Access & Security**
3. **Performance Optimization**
4. **Data Loading & Unloading**
5. **Data Transformation**
6. **Data Protection & Sharing**

---

## 📋 "Exam Details"

- **Format:** Multiple-choice and multiple-select questions  
- **Duration:** 115 minutes  
- **Total Questions:** 100  
- **Passing Score:** 750 out of 1000  
- **Style:** Mostly **scenario-based**, requiring practical understanding of Snowflake features and best practices

---

## 🎯 "Why It Matters"

- Recognized industry-wide as a **proof of Snowflake expertise**
- Helps in career advancement and job opportunities
- Builds a strong foundation for advanced Snowflake certifications

---

## 🧭 "What’s Next After SnowPro Core?"

Once you’ve earned the **SnowPro Core Certification**, you can pursue **role-specific advanced certifications** to deepen your expertise:

### 🔹 SnowPro Advanced Certifications

- **SnowPro Advanced: Architect** – Focuses on designing scalable and secure Snowflake solutions  
- **SnowPro Advanced: Data Engineer** – Covers data pipelines, transformations, and performance tuning  
- **SnowPro Advanced: Data Scientist** – Emphasizes Snowflake’s ML/AI capabilities and integrations  
- **SnowPro Advanced: Administrator** – Focuses on account management, security, and governance  

Each of these builds on the Core certification and targets specialized roles in the Snowflake ecosystem.
