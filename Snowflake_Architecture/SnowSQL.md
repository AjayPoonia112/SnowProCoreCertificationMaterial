# ❄️ SnowSQL – Command Line Client for Snowflake

SnowSQL is the **command-line tool** provided by Snowflake to interact with your account.

---

## ✅ What SnowSQL Does
- Connect to Snowflake from the command line.
- Execute **queries**, **DDL**, and **DML** commands.
- Load and unload data.
- Perform administrative tasks.

---

## 🖥️ Supported Platforms
- Windows
- macOS
- Linux

## ⚙️ Steps to Set Up SnowSQL
1. **Download SnowSQL** from the official link.
2. **Install SnowSQL** on your system.
3. **Connect to your Snowflake account** using your credentials.
4. **Run queries** and manage your account directly from the CLI.

---

Example:
```bash
snowsql -a <account_name> -u <username>
