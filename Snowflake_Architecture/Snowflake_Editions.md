## 🏷️ Snowflake Editions

> **"Snowflake offers multiple editions to provide cost-optimized features based on business scale and requirements."**  
> **"Each edition includes all features of the previous one, plus additional capabilities."**

### 1️⃣ Standard Edition
- ✅ Data Warehouse
- ✅ Automatic Data Encryption
- ✅ Broad support for standard and special data types
- ✅ Time Travel up to **1 day**
- ✅ Disaster Recovery for **7 days** beyond Time Travel
- ✅ Network Policies
- ✅ Secure Data Sharing
- ✅ Federated Authentication & SSO
- ✅ Premier Support 24/7

---

### 2️⃣ Enterprise Edition
Includes **all Standard features**, plus:
- ✅ Multi-Cluster Warehouse
- ✅ Time Travel up to **90 days**
- ✅ Materialized Views
- ✅ Search Optimization
- ✅ Column-Level Security
- ✅ 24-hour early access to weekly new releases

---

### 3️⃣ Business Critical Edition
Includes **all Enterprise features**, plus:
- ✅ Additional security features (e.g., Customer-Managed Encryption)
- ✅ Support for data-specific regulations
- ✅ Database Failover/Failback (Disaster Recovery)

---

### 4️⃣ Virtual Private Snowflake (VPS)
Includes **all Business Critical features**, plus:
- ✅ Dedicated Virtual Servers and completely separate Snowflake environment
- ✅ Dedicated Metadata Store
- ✅ Fully isolated from all other Snowflake accounts
- ✅ Is doesn't include some share features due to security issues.

---

This tiered approach ensures **cost optimization** while providing advanced features for scalability, security, and compliance.
